# Mangonur Script Style

## Voice

Mangonur narration should feel like a smart friend telling you an unbelievable story quickly.

Default characteristics:

- Turkish;
- conversational;
- compact;
- curious;
- lightly mischievous;
- informative without sounding like a lecture;
- humorous without becoming sketch comedy;
- confident only where facts are supported.

## Humor model

Prefer:

- irony;
- understatement;
- absurd comparison;
- expectation reversal;
- dry commentary;
- escalating consequences;
- short modern analogy;
- bureaucratic or political absurdity;
- characters behaving in recognizably human ways.

Avoid:

- random jokes unrelated to the story;
- excessive slang;
- jokes every sentence;
- fake quotes attributed to real people;
- invented dialogue presented as historical fact;
- humiliating victims;
- edgy humor that damages clarity.

A useful target is roughly:
- 70-80% story/information;
- 20-30% humor, rhythm, and personality.

This ratio is directional, not mathematical.

## Script architecture

### 1. Hook

The first sentence should create immediate curiosity.

Good hook patterns:

- impossible-sounding fact;
- surprising comparison;
- "this lasted less than..." framing;
- a person making one decision with huge consequences;
- a familiar object or situation with an unexpected history;
- a contradiction.

Avoid generic openings such as:

- "Bugün size..."
- "Tarihte ilginç bir olay..."
- "Biliyor muydunuz?"

unless deliberately used as a joke.

### 2. Setup

Give only the context required to understand what is happening.

Names, dates, titles, and geography should be introduced only when useful.

### 3. Escalation

Every few sentences, add one of:

- new consequence;
- reversal;
- obstacle;
- scale increase;
- absurd detail;
- important reveal.

### 4. Payoff

End with something that feels earned:

- final consequence;
- ironic outcome;
- revealing fact;
- memorable comparison;
- callback to the hook.

Do not fade out into a Wikipedia-style summary.

## Spoken-language rules

Write for the ear.

Prefer:
- short-to-medium sentences;
- contractions and natural Turkish rhythm where appropriate;
- clear referents;
- punchy transitions.

Avoid:
- dense lists of dates;
- nested clauses;
- excessive parentheticals;
- academic phrasing;
- overloaded proper nouns.

Read the script mentally as spoken audio before presenting it.

## Factual comedy

A joke may interpret a verified fact, but must not replace it.

Example principle:

Verified fact:
A conflict lasted approximately 38-45 minutes depending on how duration is defined.

Acceptable narration:
"Çay koysan, savaş bitmeden demlenmeyebilirdi."

Not acceptable:
"They literally stopped the war because tea was ready."

The second version invents causality.

## Sensitive events

When the subject includes death, war, disaster, or abuse:

- keep descriptions non-graphic unless necessary;
- do not make victims the punchline;
- humor may focus on absurd decisions, institutions, logistics, propaganda, timing, or historical irony;
- preserve basic dignity.

## Revision behavior

When the user gives feedback such as:

- "daha komik";
- "çok uzun";
- "hook zayıf";
- "burayı değiştir";
- "daha hızlı aksın";

revise the current script only.

Do not advance to shots until the revised script is approved.
