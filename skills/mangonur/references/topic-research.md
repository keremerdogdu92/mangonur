# Mangonur Topic Research

## Goal

Find topics that are both genuinely timely and unusually suitable for a short, visual, entertaining video.

The goal is not to produce a generic news digest.

## Research window

Default search window:
- primarily the last 7 days;
- expand to the last 30 days when a topic is still accelerating or has strong delayed virality.

A historical anniversary may qualify when a current event has caused renewed attention.

## Evidence of virality

Look for multiple signals when possible:

- rapid coverage by several independent outlets;
- visible social-media discussion reported or indexed by reliable sources;
- unusually high search interest;
- a meme, clip, discovery, controversy, launch, or event spreading across platforms;
- repeated explainers appearing because audiences are asking the same question;
- a strong current hook attached to an older story.

Do not fabricate:
- view counts;
- search-volume numbers;
- trending ranks;
- platform metrics.

If exact metrics are unavailable, use qualitative wording.

## Candidate scoring

Score candidates internally on five dimensions from 1-10:

1. `TIMELINESS`
   How clearly is the topic rising now?

2. `HOOK`
   Can the premise be explained in one compelling sentence?

3. `VISUALITY`
   Can the story produce several distinct, understandable images?

4. `STORY`
   Does it have setup, escalation, surprise, conflict, irony, or payoff?

5. `MANGONUR_FIT`
   Can it be factual and lightly funny without becoming forced or insensitive?

Prefer topics with balanced strength rather than one exceptional dimension and four weak ones.

## User-facing shortlist

Normally return 5-8 choices.

For each candidate include:

### [Topic title]
**Why now:** one sentence.
**Hook:** one sentence.
**Why it fits Mangonur:** one short sentence.
**Score:** X/10.

Keep this phase concise.

Do not write a complete script.

End by asking the user to choose a topic.

## Topic diversity

Unless the user has given a narrower direction, try to include variety such as:

- bizarre current event;
- history resurfacing because of current news;
- science or archaeology;
- technology or AI;
- geopolitics with a simple human-scale story;
- culture or internet phenomenon;
- unusual law, competition, invention, accident, discovery, or record.

Do not fill the list with near-duplicates.

## Selection lock

Once the user picks a topic:

- stop pitching alternatives;
- switch from discovery to verification;
- research enough context to avoid a shallow or misleading script.
